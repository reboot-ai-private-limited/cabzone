**Status:** Draft for Cabzone's legal, privacy, tax, and operations owners. Do not publish as final terms or submit policy URLs to Google Play until the decisions below are approved. ## Privacy notice: factual inventory to verify

Cabzone Rider uses a phone number and OTP for account access. Depending on what a user enters or does, the service processes rider profile details, pickup and destination locations, ride requests and history, agreed fares, payment and dispute records, saved routes, emergency contact details, and support messages.

Cabzone Driver processes phone number, profile and vehicle information, licences and other KYC documents, availability and trip locations (including background location while online or on a ride when permission is granted), ride offers and history, payment confirmations, wallet and payout records, and support messages.

These data support authentication, ride matching, navigation, safety, payment and settlement, fraud prevention, customer support, and legal compliance. Confirm actual production providers and transfers for SMS, mapping/routing, payment processing, Firebase/Expo notifications, Cloudinary document storage, realtime calling, hosting, and analytics before publishing. List each provider and whether data leaves India where required by counsel.

Users can start an account and associated-data deletion request at <https://cabzone-admin.vercel.app/account-deletion> or by emailing `cabzonehelp@gmail.com`. Operations must verify account ownership, fulfill the request, coordinate deletion with processors, and explain any records kept for legal or payment reasons. The request page and app links do not themselves erase server data.

**Decisions required before publication:** registered operator name/address; grievance contact; lawful bases and consent text; exact data categories shared with each provider; document, trip, payment, message and backup retention periods; deletion method and service level; cross-border transfers; children's age scope; privacy-policy effective date and version.

## Rider terms: product facts to verify

- A quoted route and fare are shown before a ride request; fare negotiation can change the final agreed fare before pickup.
- The final ride payment method shown in the app may be cash or provider-verified UPI. The driver confirms cash only after receiving it. A client-side UPI success message alone does not settle a ride.
- Cancellation or no-show fees may create arrears on a later ride under the configured policy. Cash can be blocked when arrears meet the approved threshold.
- Tolls and interstate permits may be payable separately at actuals where applicable; the app must disclose unavailable estimates honestly.
- The app provides ride history, a trip record labelled as a receipt, and a payment-dispute entry. The current receipt is not presented as a tax invoice.

**Decisions required:** approved fare/cancellation disclosure, refund and dispute timelines, safety and liability terms, tax-invoice format, GST treatment, permit/toll treatment, service area, effective date, legal operator name and address.

## Driver partner terms: product facts to verify

- Driver applicants provide profile, vehicle and KYC information and must be approved before receiving work.
- The app can use background location while the driver is online or on a ride when permission is granted; the permission screen and store declaration must match the actual use.
- The driver receives cash directly when cash is chosen; provider-verified online fare settlement credits the driver wallet after platform commission.
- Wallet top-ups fund platform wallet balance. Cashout and payout depend on the configured review or provider-payout workflow and available funds.

**Decisions required:** driver contract and aggregator status, commission and tax terms, wallet balance legal characterization, payout/cashout service levels, KYC retention, suspension and appeal process, insurance/permit obligations, safety support, effective date, legal operator name and address.
