## 2. What we collect

### Everyone (riders and drivers)
- **Mobile number.** Used to sign you in with a one-time code sent by SMS, and to contact you about your trips.
- **Device and app data.** A device identifier and push-notification token so we can keep one active session per account and send you ride alerts. Basic technical logs and crash information.
- **Trip data.** Pickup, drop-off and stops, route, fare, fare negotiation, status and timestamps, ratings, chat messages, and payment status.
- **Voice calls.** If you call the other person on a trip from inside the app, audio is carried by our calling provider. We do not record calls **[confirm with the calling setup]**.

### Riders
- **Name** (required) and **email** (optional).
- **Emergency contact** (optional): a name and phone number you choose to add.
- **Saved routes and recent places** you choose to save.
- **Location**, only while the app is open (see section 4).
- **Preferences:** language, distance units, city, theme.

### Drivers
- **Identity and profile:** name, date of birth, gender, address or operating city, emergency contact, and a profile photo.
- **Verification (KYC) documents:** driving licence details and photo, vehicle registration and category, and other vehicle documents you upload. Stored with our document storage provider.
- **Location**, including while the app is in the background when you are online (see section 4).
- **Earnings and wallet data:** trip earnings, platform commission, wallet recharges and cash-outs.
- **Payment details you choose to show riders**, such as your UPI QR code.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. The apps are not for children.

## 3. How we use it

- To create your account and sign you in.
- To match riders with nearby drivers, show the route, and run the trip.
- To verify drivers and keep trips safe, including pickup OTP checks.
- To process the platform commission and driver wallet.
- To send ride updates, messages and call alerts.
- To provide support, investigate payment disputes and prevent fraud or misuse.
- To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
- To fix bugs and improve the apps.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for third-party advertising **[confirm]**.

## 4. Location

- **Rider app:** precise location is used **only while the app is open**, to suggest your pickup point and show nearby drivers. The rider app does not collect location in the background.
- **Driver app:** precise location is used while you are **online**, including when the app is in the background or the screen is off, so riders can be matched to you and can follow the trip. The app shows a notification while this is running. Location sharing stops when you go offline.
- You can turn location off in your phone settings. The apps cannot match rides without it.

## 5. Camera, photos, microphone and notifications

- **Camera and photo library (driver app):** only when you take or choose your profile photo or upload documents.
- **Microphone:** only during an in-app call.
- **Notifications:** ride offers, status changes, messages and call alerts. You can turn these off in phone settings, but you may miss rides.

## 6. Payments

Riders pay the agreed fare **directly to the driver**, in cash or by UPI. Cabzone does not collect the fare. Drivers pay Cabzone a platform commission through their driver wallet. Wallet recharges are processed by our payment partner **Razorpay**; we do not store your card or bank credentials.

## 7. Who we share data with

- **The other person on your trip.** Riders and drivers see what is needed to complete the trip: first name or name, photo (drivers), vehicle details (drivers), pickup and drop-off, live location during the trip, rating, and chat or call.
- **Service providers** who process data for us:
  - Google Maps Platform (maps, place search, routes)
  - Cloudinary (storing profile photos and verification documents)
  - LiveKit (in-app voice calls)
  - Razorpay (driver wallet payments)
  - SMS gateway provider **[MSG91 / Twilio / other]** (sign-in codes)
  - Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)
  - Our cloud hosting provider **[provider, region]**
- **Authorities and others** where the law requires, or to protect the safety and rights of users and Cabzone.

Providers may process data outside India. We require appropriate safeguards.
