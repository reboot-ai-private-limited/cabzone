Cabzone Driver is for independent cab drivers who want ride requests, clear fares and a simple record of what they earn. Go online when you are ready to drive, see each request before you accept it, and keep the fare you agree with the rider.

RIDE OFFERS YOU CAN READ AT A GLANCE
• See the pickup distance, route and fare before you accept.
• Accept the offer, ignore it, or send a counter fare within the allowed range.
• Your estimated earnings after the platform fee are shown on every offer.

GO ONLINE WHEN YOU WANT
• One switch to go online or offline.
• Stay in your service zone to receive offers.
• Live status shows that your location is being shared while you are online.

EARN AND GET PAID DIRECTLY
• Riders pay you the agreed fare directly, in cash or by UPI.
• Show your UPI QR code at the end of the trip and confirm payment inside the app.
• Cabzone charges an 8% platform commission, recorded against your driver wallet.

CLEAR EARNINGS
• Net earnings for today, the last 7 days, the last 30 days or all time.
• Gross fare, platform fee and trip counts in one place.
• Full ledger of commission debits and wallet top-ups.

ALL YOUR TRIPS IN ONE PLACE
• Active trip, live offers and complete ride history under Rides.
• Filter by completed, cancelled or disputed trips.
• Rate riders after a completed trip.

TALK TO YOUR RIDER
• Chat with the rider about the pickup.
• Call the rider from inside the app.

VERIFIED AND TRUSTED
• Complete KYC once: personal details, driving licence, vehicle details and documents, and a profile photo.
• Get reminders before documents expire, so you can renew without losing rides.
• Riders see your name, photo and vehicle at pickup.

SUPPORT
• Call or email the driver support desk from Profile > Help.
• Report a payment dispute with your trip ID and our team reviews the trip.

PERMISSIONS
• Location (including while the app is in the background): needed while you are online so riders can be matched to you and can follow the trip. Sharing stops when you go offline.
• Camera and photos: only when you upload your profile photo and documents.
• Microphone: only for in-app calls with riders.
• Notifications: new ride offers, rider messages and call alerts.

REQUIREMENTS
You need a valid driving licence, a registered vehicle and completed KYC verification before you can go online.

Cabzone is currently available in and around Siliguri, West Bengal. Light and dark themes are supported.
